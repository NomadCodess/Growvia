DIAGNOSTIC BENCH First Aid for Houseplants & Garden Crops

Plant Doctor Quick-Troubleshooter

Ask the Community

Notice something off with your foliage or potting soil? Select a symptom below for rapid diagnostic checks and proven organic remedies:

Lower leaves turning pale yellow while stems feel soft?

Primary Cause: Moisture saturation around the root ball leading to oxygen starvation, or natural shedding of old lower leaves.

Recommended Action:

Test soil depth 2 inches down with a clean wooden chopstick. If damp or cool, withhold water completely for 7–10 days. Ensure the drainage hole isn't blocked by sitting saucer water. If leaves are uniformly light green, dilute a teaspoon of liquid vermicompost extract to replenish nitrogen.

Diagnostic Check
  • Chopstick test: Top 50% soil must be dry
  • Pot weight: Heavy pot = trapped water
  • Browse Watering Guides →
Edges and leaf tips are dry, papery, or curled inward?

Primary Cause: Low ambient room humidity (under 40%), tap water mineral/fluoride salt burn, or erratic under-watering.

Recommended Action:

Group tropical potted plants together to create a natural transpiration microclimate. Flush the pot with rainwater or filtered water until runoff flows freely to leach accumulated fertilizer salts. Trim crispy brown tips leaving a tiny sliver of margin to avoid opening fresh wounds.

Diagnostic Check
  • Water source: Switch to filtered or rested water
  • Pebble tray: Keep humidity 50%+ for aroids
  • Search Community Symptoms →
White, talcum-like powder spreading on upper leaf surfaces and buds?

Primary Cause: Fungal powdery mildew (Podosphaera pannosa) triggered by stagnant warm air and dense foliage.

Recommended Action:

Prune dense center stems to improve air circulation. Spray early in the morning with a gentle bio-fungicide: dissolve 1 teaspoon potassium bicarbonate (or baking soda) and 2 drops of castile soap into 1 Liter warm water. Never wet foliage in late evening.

Diagnostic Check
  • Ventilation: Space pots at least 6 inches apart
  • Bio-spray: Apply every 5 days for 2 weeks
  • Organic Disease Control Tips →
Tiny black flies hovering over moist potting soil or seedlings collapsing?

Primary Cause: Fungus gnat larvae feeding on decaying organic matter in perpetually soggy topsoil.

Recommended Action:

Allow top 2 inches of soil to dry out bone-dry to break the egg cycle. Dust food-grade Ceylon cinnamon powder over the soil surface (a natural organic anti-fungal that eliminates larvae food sources). Water from the bottom saucer rather than from the top.

Diagnostic Check
  • Topsoil barrier: Top with 1/2 inch coarse sand
  • Bottom watering: 15 mins in shallow tray

Gardener's Calendar

Seasonal Cultivation Almanac

Timely tasks for current climate cycles
Soil & Compost Refresh

Gently aerate compacted container soil with a hand fork. Top-dress with 1-2 inches of screened vermicompost to replenish micronutrients without disturbing roots.

Clean Shears & Prune

Sterilize pruners in 70% alcohol. Trim leggy vines above leaf nodes to stimulate bushier branching and prepare node cuttings for propagation.

Foliar Pest Defense

Mist leaf undersides with cold-pressed neem oil (5ml/L with mild castile soap) every 10 days at sunset to deter spider mites and thrips before colonies take hold.

Sunlight Angle Check

As seasonal sun arcs shift, rotate houseplants a quarter turn weekly. Move heat-sensitive seedlings to bright indirect light to prevent leaf scorch.
